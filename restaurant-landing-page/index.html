Our Story

Honest ingredients, handled with care.

LUMIÈRE began with a simple idea: a table where nobody is rushed and every plate tells you where it came from. We cook with seasonal produce from nearby growers and let each ingredient speak for itself.

Our dining philosophy is hospitality first. Whether it is a quiet dinner for two, a family celebration or a business evening, we want you to leave feeling looked after.

  • Seasonal menus that change with the harvest
  • Crafted from scratch in our open kitchen
  • Welcoming service for every occasion
